# Phishing Email Identification

## Objective
The objective of this exercise is to identify phishing emails using free online cybersecurity tools.

## Tools Used
- VirusTotal
- Google Safe Browsing
- PhishTank
- MXToolbox

## Signs of Phishing Emails

1. Suspicious sender email address
2. Urgent language such as "verify your account immediately"
3. Links that lead to fake websites
4. Requests for sensitive information
5. Spelling or grammar errors

## Example Scenario

Example phishing email:

Subject: Your account has been suspended

Message:
Dear User,

We noticed unusual activity on your account. Please verify your account immediately by clicking the link below.

http://fake-paypal-security.com

This email is suspicious because:
- The domain name is not official
- The message creates urgency
- It asks the user to click a suspicious link

## Conclusion

Phishing attacks remain one of the most common cyber threats. Users must carefully verify email senders, avoid clicking suspicious links, and use security tools to check potentially malicious messages.
